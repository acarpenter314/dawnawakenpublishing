About Dawn Awaken Publishing

Dawn Awaken Publishing was founded by poet and developer Allan Carpenter in Richmond, Indiana. The imprint began as a creative experiment—an intersection of technology, poetry, and mythic storytelling.

Our mission is to publish works that illuminate the unseen and awaken the timeless. We believe literature should challenge, inspire, and connect readers to the deeper rhythms of existence.

Our Story

Born from a lifelong love of words and world-building, Dawn Awaken Publishing grew out of Allan’s vision to create a space for independent voices exploring transformation, identity, and myth. Each publication reflects a balance between modern innovation and ancient resonance.

Our Commitment

We are dedicated to authenticity, creative freedom, and the pursuit of artistic excellence. Every book we produce is crafted with care and reverence for the creative process.
