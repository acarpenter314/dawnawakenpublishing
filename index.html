Dawn Awaken Publishing

Independent Publisher of Poetry and Mythic Fiction At Dawn Awaken Publishing, we believe that words carry the power to illuminate the unseen and awaken the timeless. Founded by poet and developer Allan Carpenter, our imprint stands at the intersection of art and imagination—where mythic storytelling meets modern expression. Our Vision We publish works that explore transformation, identity, and the cycles of creation. Each poem, story, and collection we release seeks to bridge the ancient and the contemporary, the personal and the cosmic. Our Focus Poetry Collections that evoke emotion, rhythm, and reflection. Mythic Fiction that reimagines archetypes and legends for a new generation. Creative Experiments that blend technology, narrative, and art. Our Philosophy We see publishing as a living art form—a dialogue between creator and reader. Every book we produce is crafted with care, authenticity, and a reverence for the creative process.
